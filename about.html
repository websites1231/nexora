About WEBSKY

An independent information website focused on making digital topics easier to understand.

Who we are

WEBSKY is an India-based website that publishes general information about technology, digital tools and online safety. We aim to present topics in straightforward language and help readers find relevant primary sources for further research.

What we do

We create educational and informational website content covering online safety awareness, common digital risks, technology concepts and publicly available product information. Our content is intended to support research and general understanding.

Our approach

  • Clarity: explain topics in accessible language.
  • Transparency: distinguish our independent content from third-party brands and services.
  • Accuracy: direct readers to official sources for current product terms, pricing and availability.
  • Privacy: explain what information may be collected when visitors contact us.
WEBSKY is independent and is not owned, operated, sponsored or endorsed by Guardio or its parent company. References to third-party names are for identification and informational purposes.
